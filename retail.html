Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Retail POS

Can this handle a busy supermarket checkout?

Yes. Our retail POS software Dubai and Ajman businesses use is optimized for speed. Barcode scanning and receipt printing happen instantly, keeping the queue moving fast.

Does it work when the internet is down?

Yes. TillEase is a hybrid POS system (cloud & offline). You can continue scanning, selling, and printing VAT-compliant invoices without an internet connection.

Can I manage inventory across multiple shops?

Absolutely. You can track stock levels across multiple branches in the UAE, allowing you to easily transfer inventory and monitor sales centrally.

Do you offer complete supermarket POS hardware setup?

Yes, we provide end-to-end hardware implementation. Our team will install the retail POS software alongside heavy-duty barcode scanners, cash drawers, and receipt printers to ensure your checkout is ready from day one.

Can multiple checkout lanes share the same inventory database?

Yes. You can run multiple till terminals in the same supermarket connected to a single local database. This means stock counts update instantly across all registers, even if the internet goes down.
