The Architecture

Hybrid: Local Speed meets Cloud Scale

Terminals talk to each other locally in-store. If the internet goes out, your store stays in sync. When it comes back online, the local server pushes everything to the cloud automatically. Your Cloud Dashboard acts as your central command, giving you access to real-time business analytics and live reporting from any device, anywhere in the world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions for Small Businesses

Does the POS work without internet in the UAE?

Yes. TillEase is designed as an offline-first POS software. You can continue ringing up sales, printing receipts, and managing your queue even if your internet connection in Dubai, Sharjah, or Ajman completely drops. Data syncs automatically once you are back online.

Is TillEase VAT compliant in the UAE?

Absolutely. Our POS system generates fully FTA VAT-compliant invoices, including accurate tax breakdowns on every receipt, ensuring your business software solutions meet UAE regulatory standards.

Can I manage multiple branches across different emirates?

Yes. Whether you have a shop in Ajman and another in Abu Dhabi, TillEase centralizes your inventory, sales data, and reporting into a single dashboard while still running flawlessly locally at each branch.

Do you provide POS hardware and implementation in the UAE?

Yes. We don't just provide software; we handle full turnkey hardware integration. We supply, install, and implement the complete POS system—including touchscreens, receipt printers, and barcode scanners—directly at your location in Dubai, Ajman, or anywhere in the UAE.

Can we run multiple POS terminals in the same store?

Absolutely. Our offline-first architecture allows you to connect multiple terminals to the same local database within your store. All registers sync perfectly in real-time without relying on an external internet connection.
