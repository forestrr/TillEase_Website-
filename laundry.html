Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Laundry Management

How much does laundry Management software cost in UAE?

Pricing varies based on your branch count and whether you need the driver app. However, TillEase offers an upfront license fee with transparent AMC support plans. Check our Pricing page for tailored quotes.

Is it a multi-branch laundry software for UAE?

Yes. TillEase is designed to sync data perfectly across multiple locations, making it ideal for growing dry cleaning businesses with a central washing facility and multiple drop-off shops.

Does the driver app come included?

The driver app is available as part of our Growth package, tailored specifically for laundry POS systems that handle delivery and pickup routes directly.

Do you provide hardware installation for laundry shops?

Yes. We provide full hardware integration, setting up your touchscreen tills and specialized tag printers so you can start tracking garments immediately without sourcing your own equipment.

Can multiple counters share the same database?

Yes, larger laundry hubs can run multiple terminals connected to the same local database, keeping drop-offs, pickups, and driver assignments perfectly in sync in real-time.
