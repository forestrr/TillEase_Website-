How Much Does POS Software Actually Cost in the UAE?

Monthly subscriptions quietly drain your margins. One-time licenses look expensive upfront. Here is the real math — and what Reddit business owners wish they knew before signing.

6 min read
August 2026
Mohammed Afif

One of the most common questions UAE small business owners ask on Reddit and community forums is: "How much should I actually pay for a POS system?" The answer is frustratingly vague on most vendor websites. This guide gives you real numbers.

Two Pricing Models: Subscription vs One-Time License

POS software in the UAE market follows two main pricing structures:

Monthly / Annual Subscription (SaaS)

You pay a recurring fee — typically AED 150 to AED 500 per month per terminal. The vendor hosts the software in the cloud and handles updates. Sounds convenient, but the cost compounds quickly:

  • AED 300/month = AED 3,600/year = AED 10,800 over 3 years
  • Add a second terminal? Double that.
  • Stop paying? You lose access to your own sales data.

One-Time Perpetual License

You pay once — typically AED 2,000 to AED 8,000 depending on the industry and feature set — and the software is yours. Updates and support are usually covered by an optional Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) at a fraction of the original cost.

  • AED 5,000 one-time + AED 1,000/year AMC = AED 7,000 over 3 years
  • No per-terminal surprise fees
  • Your data stays on your machine — always accessible

The Reddit consensus: "I wish someone had told me that my AED 250/month POS subscription would cost me more than AED 9,000 in 3 years — when a one-time license would have been half that." — paraphrased from multiple r/dubai and r/smallbusiness threads.

Hidden Fees That Catch Business Owners Off Guard

The sticker price on a POS website rarely tells the full story. Watch for:

  • Per-user fees — some systems charge per staff login on top of the terminal fee
  • Payment gateway cuts — a percentage on every card transaction routed through the POS provider's processor
  • Hardware lock-in — proprietary tablets or printers that only work with that vendor's software
  • Data export fees — wanting to leave? Some vendors charge to export your own transaction history
  • Feature gating — inventory management, multi-branch, or VAT reports locked behind a "premium" tier

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does POS software cost in the UAE?

POS software in the UAE ranges from AED 150-500/month for cloud subscriptions to AED 2,000-8,000 for a one-time perpetual license. Total cost depends on the number of terminals, features included, and whether hardware is bundled.

Is a monthly POS subscription or one-time license cheaper long-term?

For businesses planning to operate for more than 18-24 months, a one-time license is almost always cheaper. A AED 300/month subscription costs AED 10,800 over 3 years, while a one-time license of AED 5,000 saves over AED 5,000 in the same period.

What hidden fees should I watch for with POS software?

Common hidden costs include per-terminal add-on fees, payment gateway transaction percentages, mandatory hardware bundles, premium support charges, and fees for additional user accounts or branches.

Bottom Line

Do the 3-year math before you sign anything. A POS that looks cheap at AED 200/month costs AED 7,200 — and that is before add-ons. A one-time license with a predictable AMC almost always wins on total cost of ownership for UAE small businesses.
