Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Restaurant POS

Does it integrate directly with Talabat and Deliveroo?

Yes. TillEase offers POS software with Talabat integration and other major delivery platforms, pulling orders directly into your kitchen display system without needing separate tablets for each app.

Can my waiters take orders at the table?

Absolutely. Our waiter app allows staff to take orders tableside using a mobile device, instantly sending the ticket to the kitchen and updating the main till.

Is the software VAT compliant in the UAE?

Yes, every receipt generated is fully FTA VAT-compliant, ensuring your cafe or restaurant meets all UAE tax regulations effortlessly.

Do you provide restaurant POS hardware and kitchen printers?

Yes, we handle full hardware integration. We supply and implement the touchscreen tills for the floor, rugged kitchen display screens, and receipt printers, ensuring everything communicates seamlessly.

Can we have multiple waiter stations in the same cafe?

Yes. You can deploy multiple POS terminals and waiter apps connected to the same local database. All orders sync instantly to the kitchen and main till without needing a cloud connection.
