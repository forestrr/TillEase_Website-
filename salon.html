Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Salon POS

Does the software handle staff commission?

Yes. TillEase acts as dedicated staff commission tracking software for salons. It allows you to assign specific rates to different stylists or services and calculates payroll automatically at the end of the week.

Can it manage spa packages and memberships?

Absolutely. As a complete spa POS system in the UAE, you can manage client balances, track prepaid sessions, and automatically deduct from their package when they check out.

Is it VAT compliant for the UAE?

Yes. Every receipt you print is 100% FTA VAT-compliant, protecting your salon or spa from compliance issues.

Do you supply salon POS hardware and cash registers?

Yes, we offer full hardware implementation. We will install sleek POS terminals and receipt printers that match your salon's aesthetic, providing a complete turnkey setup.

Can we run multiple reception desks on the same database?

Absolutely. If you have a busy spa with multiple receptionists, you can connect multiple terminals to the same local database, ensuring appointment bookings and staff commissions sync instantly across all screens.
